# Using Estate Inventory

A short guide for everyone in the family. You can send this page to anyone you invite.

## Getting started

1. Open the invitation link you were sent, on your phone.
2. **Add the app to your Home Screen before signing in.**
   - iPhone: in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen". Open the app from the new icon.
   - Android: in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Install app".
   This matters: the app keeps your work safe on the phone when there's no signal, and iPhone only does that reliably for apps on the Home Screen.
3. Sign in with the password your administrator gave you. Email sign-in codes are not configured on the hosted app yet.
4. If you weren't taken to the estate, choose Join and enter the invitation code from your message.

## Recording an item

Tap **Add item**.

1. Take a photo (or several: the whole item, any markings, serial numbers, damage).
2. Give it a short description and pick a category. "Miscellaneous" is fine if you're unsure.
3. Say which room it was found in and where it is now. If you're not putting it anywhere yet, leave it in the Holding area.
4. Tick "Might be valuable or collectible" if it could be worth getting appraised. Tick "Hold in place" for anything that must not be moved, sold or separated from its set.
5. If the app asks **"Is this already in the inventory?"**, look at the photos it shows:
   - "It's the same item": someone already recorded it. Your photos are added to theirs.
   - "Another one": there really are two (or more). The count goes up.
   - Otherwise, it's a different item.
6. Choose what you'd like to happen to it, or skip that for later.

Each item gets a code like `0428KB-LR7FUR-Q3X`. You'll see it on labels and reports; you never need to type it.

**Scanning barcodes.** On the add screen, scan a product barcode to fill in what the app can find.

**Photos you took earlier.** Use **Choose photos (taken earlier)** to pick several at once from your photo library, for example ones you took with no signal.

**Markings and inscriptions.** Under **More details**, write down any maker's mark, signature, engraving or hallmark, and use **Add a photo of the marking** so the family can see it. On an item's page, **Photo of a marking** does the same.

## No signal? Keep going

Everything works without a connection: adding items, photos, scanning, moving things between boxes, and making requests. The bar at the top tells you what's waiting:

- **Offline: 5 waiting** means five changes are saved on your phone and will upload when you're back online.
- **5 to upload** means you're online and they're on their way.

Open the app somewhere with signal now and then so it can upload. **Upload queue** (under More) shows exactly what's still on your phone. Don't delete the app while anything is waiting.

If an upload or screen fails, open **More > Upload queue > Technical details for troubleshooting**. Copy those details and send them to the estate administrator. They include the app version, connection state and recent error codes, not passwords or sign-in tokens.

## Boxes, rooms and staging areas

Under **More > Places and labels** you'll find every room, box and staging area (like "Donate pile" or "To Brittany's car"). Each has a printable label with a QR code.

## Many items at once: Batch inventory

**Batch inventory** (on the home screen) is for clearing a shelf or a room quickly:

1. Set what the items have in common: the room they were found in, where they are going (a box or staging area), the kind of item, the brand, the condition.
2. Add items to the running list by scanning barcodes, typing names, or taking or choosing photos (each photo becomes one item).
3. Give any item its own name, quantity, extra photos, or a different kind or brand.
4. Tap **Save**. Every item gets its code; you can print labels for the whole batch.

Scanning a box's label sets where the batch is going. Scanning an existing item's label adds it to the batch as a move, so packing a box is: scan the box, scan each item, save. It all works with no signal.

## Deciding where things go

Open an item to see what can happen next.

- **Give to a family member**: propose that it goes to you or someone else. Depending on the estate's rules, the executor approves it, or everyone does, or a majority. You can't approve something given to yourself.
- **Up for grabs: ask everyone**: everyone says whether they're interested. If the estate uses **sealed** choices, nobody (administrators included) can see anyone else's answer until the reveal time. Then all answers appear at once. If two people want it, the family decides, or the app runs a draw that anyone can check afterwards.
- **Sell, donate, recycle, throw away**: proposed and approved the same way.

**Decisions** shows everything waiting for your approval and what's been decided.

## Mistakes

Nothing in the app can be deleted, by anyone. That's on purpose: it's what lets everyone trust the record. Instead:

- Wrong details? Edit the item. The old version stays in the history.
- Photo uploaded by mistake? Open it and choose "Hide this photo (uploaded by mistake)". It disappears from view for everyone.
- Approved something by mistake? Ask an estate administrator to "Set aside" your decision before it becomes final. The reason is recorded too.

## Keeping your own copy

**More > Save a copy** makes a single file with every item, decision and the full history, plus small photos (or all photos, if you tick the box). On iPhone, choose "Save to Files", then iCloud Drive. The file opens on any computer without the app: it includes a readable inventory page and spreadsheet files.

The same screen shows the **History check**. Your phone keeps a fingerprint of the shared history and checks that nothing already recorded has changed. If it ever warns you that the history doesn't match, take a screenshot and tell the estate administrator.

## Reports and labels

**More > Reports** lists the full inventory, values, who received what, open issues (exceptions), and the activity history. Tap one to read it on your phone, then **Save PDF** or **Spreadsheet**. On iPhone, if the share sheet doesn't open by itself, tap **Save or share** in the "Your file is ready" box. Label sheets print on Avery 5160 (items) and 5163 (places).

## Getting help

The question mark at the top of each screen explains that screen and has a button to call the estate administrator.
